How does the AI auto-reschedule my day?

If a calendar event runs late, Sortia's predictive engine instantly shifts your remaining tasks to the next available slot automatically.

Which calendars does Sortia sync with?

We feature full, real-time two-way synchronization with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook to completely prevent double-booking.

Do I have to format my voice notes?

Not at all. Speak entirely naturally into the capture bar; our advanced NLP automatically extracts deadlines, context, and action items.

Is my calendar data safe?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard, end-to-end encryption to read your schedule, and your personal data is never sold or shared.
