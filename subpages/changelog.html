Changelog

What's new in Sortia.

Keep track of our latest features, improvements, and fixes as we continuously build the ultimate scheduling engine.

04-07-2026

Version 1.2.0

Sortia for Teams & Advanced AI Prioritization

Sortia is now built for collaborative environments. We have introduced shared workspace syncing and upgraded our intelligent engine to automatically flag your most critical daily tasks.


  • Shared Workspace Sync: Connect Sortia to your team’s shared calendars. The engine now automatically cross-references your colleagues' mandatory syncs and proactively protects your solo deep-work blocks from being double-booked by teammates.

  • AI Priority Flagging: Struggling with decision paralysis? The Sortia AI now actively cuts through your master to-do list and flags the top three high-impact tasks you need to execute each morning.

  • Smart Push Notifications & Live Activities: Track the remaining time in your current focus block right from your iOS Lock Screen using Live Activities. Receive instant push notifications when a meeting runs over and your afternoon schedule requires your review.

01-07-2026

Version 1.1.0

The Auto-Rescheduling Engine & Offline Capture

This update introduces our most requested feature: the ability for Sortia to actively manage the chaos of a changing schedule in real-time, even when you are disconnected.


  • Auto-Rescheduling Engine: If a calendar event runs late, Sortia’s predictive engine now instantly shifts your remaining tasks to the next available slot automatically. No more manual dragging and dropping to fix a broken afternoon timeline.

  • Offline Voice Capture: You can now drop chaotic voice memos or text streams into the capture bar without an internet connection. Sortia securely stores your mind dump and instantly runs the AI optimization to extract deadlines and context the moment you reconnect.

  • Dark Mode Optimization: We’ve overhauled the entire UI with a true-black dark mode, optimizing battery life for iOS users and creating a distraction-free visual environment for your Task Dashboard.

05-06-2026

Version 1.0.0

Welcome to Sortia: Public Beta Launch

Sortia for iOS is officially available. Launch the app and let our intelligent engine build your daily timeline so you can trade calendar anxiety for effortless execution.


  • Voice-to-Action Capture Bar: Speak entirely naturally into the app. Our advanced NLP automatically extracts deadlines, context, and action items from your messy, stream-of-consciousness thoughts and turns them into structured data.

  • Universal Calendar Sync: Full, real-time two-way synchronization is now live for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Microsoft Outlook. Sortia reads your existing commitments and builds your task blocks around them.

  • The Smart Task Dashboard: Your daily calendar blocks instantly adjust to give you a perfectly prioritized, stress-free timeline. View your entire day at a glance with our clean, uncluttered interface.
